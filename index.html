Hi baby!

Happy 8th Monthsary to us. I’m really grateful for everything we’ve shared these past months—the good days, the random trips, the laughs, and even the little arguments that somehow teach us more about each other.

I know sometimes you feel bad because I’m the one who drives, visits you, or spends on our dates, but please don’t feel guilty about that. I do those things because I want to spend time with you. I enjoy being with you, and for me, the memories we make together are worth more than anything I spend.

And please don’t overthink that one day I’ll get tired, lose interest, or find someone “better.” Relationships aren’t always perfect, and yes, may times na mapapagod or maiinis tayo sa isa’t isa, but that doesn’t mean I want to leave. I’m still here because I choose you.

I’m sorry too for the times na sumasabay yung pride or galit ko. We both have things to work on, but I hope we keep choosing to fix things instead of giving up on each other.

You also don’t have to feel like you need to “pay me back.” Your time, your effort, your messages, and simply having you beside me already mean a lot. No pressure, no need to rush anything.

Thank you for staying, for all the memories, and for being part of my life. I’m looking forward to more months, more years, more road trips, more food trips, more random moments, and more memories with you.

Happy 8th Monthsary, baby. I love you so much. ♡

Love, Christian
